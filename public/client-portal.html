👥 How referrals work

15%

The standard referrer share is calculated from confirmed client-paid money only. It is recorded against the TTG transaction after payment truth is confirmed.

  • Referral commission is based on money actually received from the client.
  • The fixed referrer rate is 15% in the current referral contract.
  • A separate 5% goodwill reserve may be controlled by TTG for approved bonuses, discounts, cashback or other goodwill uses; it is not automatically owed to the referrer.
  • Commission status follows the underlying payment record and payout workflow.

🚚 Delivery / Transit Estimates

These are route guidelines after the shipping company receives and dispatches the parcel. Seller processing before carrier handoff is separate.
